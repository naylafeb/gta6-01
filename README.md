[![rank](https://make.fun/badge/gta6-01/naylafeb/gta6-01/rank.svg)](https://make.fun/bounty/gta6-01)
[![reward](https://make.fun/badge/gta6-01/naylafeb/gta6-01/reward.svg)](https://make.fun/bounty/gta6-01)

An original 3D open-world crime game for the [Make GTA 6](https://make.fun/bounty/gta6-01) bounty on make.fun: a sun-soaked coastal city at dusk, a five-star wanted level, and a dramatic police chase with cars, a helicopter and a tank — with a full HUD (mission card, wanted stars, Solmar PD dispatch, live minimap, speedometer).

Round 2 rebuilds the game in 3D on a vendored three.js: third-person driving and walking, traffic and pedestrians, five story jobs, combat, and the same Solmar Bay, Jax Calloway, Lena and Brick as round 1.

Play it: serve `game/` with any static HTTP server and open `game/index.html` in desktop Chrome. WASD drive &amp; walk, E get in/out of a car, F punch, click shoot, Space handbrake, P pause.
